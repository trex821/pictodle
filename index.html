      How to play

      1. A single pictogram from a Just Dance map is shown in black and white.
      2. Search for the song and pick it from the list. You get 6 guesses.
      3. Each wrong guess or skip reveals a hint: difficulty, picto color, the coaches' genders, game, then song year.
      4. Your rating depends on how many guesses you needed: YEAH! (1), PERFECT (2), SUPER (3–4), GOOD (5), OK (6) or X.

      Daily is the same picto for everyone each day. Unlimited deals a new random picto every round. Maps come from the classic and alternate routines of Just Dance 1 through 2026, Unlimited, Decades of Hits and Just Dance+.

      Tips

      • Classic and alternate routines are separate answers. For example, “Good Feeling” and “Good Feeling - Extreme Version” are different maps.
      • Turn on Hard Mode in Settings (⚙) to play with no hints. It can only be changed before your first guess or after the round ends.

      Settings

      Hard ModeNo hints are revealed after wrong guesses or skips. All hints show once the round ends.
